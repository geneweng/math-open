TESTING THE SCOPE OF THE PROFILE CONDITIONS
29 October 2026

The original conjecture is still unresolved. This continuation DISPROVES
universality of the previous ten-profile condition and two successive
extensions. The counterexamples are to those sufficient conditions, not
to the original half bound. Their cubic graphs satisfy the original bound.

1. Exact conversion from a weighted core to a cubic graph

Let H be a connected simple graph whose vertices have degree two or three.
Let b be its number of degree-three vertices and D its number of degree-two
vertices. Attach a P13 cap by a bridge at each degree-two vertex, obtaining
the cubic graph C(H). Its order is b+14D, with half-order b/2+7D.

Every selected cycle avoids the attachment bridges. A selected degree-two
core vertex contributes one vertex but forces its cap from optimum 7 to
root-avoiding optimum 6; these contributions cancel. An unselected core
vertex permits 7 vertices in its cap. Therefore
  c_ind(C(H)) = 7D + max{|S intersect B| : H[S] is 2-regular},
where B is the set of degree-three core vertices.

The same identity holds for each five-state boundary capacity at a core
edge e after deleting e. The open state is a path between the two core
ports together with cycles. Such a path cannot enter a cap through its
only bridge and then return to the core. Hence attachment bridges are
unused in all five states, and the same cancellation applies.

Consequently the normalized boundary profile of C(H)-e is exactly the
weighted boundary profile of H-e, counting selected degree-three core
vertices and subtracting b/2. This is an exact identity, not a relaxation.
It permits a small-core search for genuine original counterexamples in
much larger cubic graphs.

The first profile dominates none of the ten vectors from ring_closure.
The second is outside the first 15-vector extension obtained by adding
the first example. The third is outside the subsequent 15-vector
extension obtained by also adding the second example.

Exact original-parent and edge-profile values are:
  n=62: c_ind=34, U=34, T=30, W=30;
  n=64: c_ind=34, U=34, T=32, W=30;
  n=64: c_ind=33, U=33, T=32, W=31.
The required original bounds are 31,32,32 respectively. Thus none is
an original counterexample. All five capacities of each exceptional
piece are independently checked by enumerating every vertex subset of
its core (orders ten, twelve, and twelve). The conversion identity above
gives the exact completed-graph optima. Full graph6 strings and selected
sets are retained in verified_examples.json.

The earlier conditional ring theorem remains valid: pieces that satisfy
its condition still give safe assemblies. What fails is the additional
claim that all realizable pieces satisfy that condition. It would be
incorrect to continue trying to prove that universal claim.

3. Direct search against the original counterexample conditions

We exhaustively generated the following two degree sequences with geng:
  12 core vertices, 16 edges: 8 degree-three and 4 degree-two vertices;
  14 core vertices, 19 edges: 10 degree-three and 4 degree-two vertices.
There are 1,892 and 21,434 connected simple cores respectively, for a
total of 23,326. Their completed cubic parents have orders 64 and 66.
This is NOT a census of all cubic graphs of orders 64 or 66.

At every core edge, the scanner computed all five normalized capacities
using the core cycle space. In a subcubic graph an even edge set is a
disjoint union of cycles. Its support is induced exactly when it has no
extra selected chord. A single extra chord gives precisely a closed-11
state after deleting that chord. Removing a selected cycle edge gives
the open-22 state. These cases exhaust all five states and justify the
enumerator.

All 437,518 distinguished core-edge cases passed both rooted tests:
  max(2U,U+T+1)>=1,
  max(2 max(U,T+2), W+3+max(U,T+2))>=3,
where U,T,W in these inequalities are normalized by half the parent
order. These are exactly the half-bound conditions for the doubled
subdivision and doubled root-triangle constructions. Every completed
parent also has a directly certified half-sized good set.

An independent verifier regenerated the entire input list, checked the
core witnesses, and checked 1,706,412 positive boundary witnesses. The
exclusions need only these lower-bound witnesses, not the scanner's
claims of optimality. Input enumeration completeness relies on geng.
Edges lying wholly inside attached caps were not part of this census.

4. Complete closure for the enlarged family

Adding the third exceptional piece produces a final 16-vector basis.
Its complete multiplication certificate is in closure_table.txt. Every
ordered product of two basis vectors dominates another basis vector,
every basis vector has nonnegative closing value, and port reversal
preserves the condition. All 256 product inequalities are independently
checked. The two intermediate 15-vector closures were checked as well.

Moreover, the independently checked lower-bound profile at every core
edge in the census dominates a final basis vector. By the monotone series
composition induction proved in ring_closure/RESULTS.txt, arbitrary ring
assemblies of these pieces satisfy the original half bound. This covers
unbounded ring length, arbitrary order, and either orientation of each
piece. The retained older pieces and families remain covered as well.

This is a conditional infinite-family theorem. Universality of the final
16-vector condition has NOT been proved. Neither the failed earlier
conditions nor the successful finite closure justify assuming it.
The random discovery runs are not completeness arguments; one later
sampling run ended at its time guard and excludes no additional region.

5. Remaining work and reproduction

No counterexample to the original conjecture was found, and no argument
establishes the half bound for arbitrary cubic graphs. The exact rooted
bad-profile target is still not excluded in general. These results must
not be described as a solution or as a new unrestricted lower bound.

From the repository root:
  python3 cubic_conjecture_search/profile_scope/verify.py

To regenerate the core certificates, compile weighted_core_scan.cpp with
clang++ -O3 -std=c++17, generate the two input files using
  geng -c -d2 -D3 12 16:16
  geng -c -d2 -D3 14 19:19
and run the executable on those files in that order. Compress its JSONL
output as core_certificates.jsonl.gz. discover_outside.py reproduces the
first randomized discovery; discover_closure.py regenerates the final
finite closure. Verification requires no optimization solver.
The paper and PDF remain unchanged.
