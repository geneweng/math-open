DIRECT EDGE-PROFILE CONTINUATION
29 October 2026

Status: the original conjecture has not been proved or disproved. These
are targeted exclusions and a two-edge-cut composition argument, not a
global reduction of arbitrary cubic graphs to the tested families.

1. Target and notation

For a cubic parent G of order 2h and an edge e=uv, let
  U=c_ind(G-e),
  T=max{|S|: S good in G and u,v in S},
  W=c_ind(G-{u,v}).
The minimum rooted triangle obstruction from the earlier reduction
requires U=h+1, T<=h-1, W<=h-2. This would yield an original counterexample
on 4h+6 vertices. No such profile was found in this continuation.

For the rooted subdivision, doubling gives exact optimum
  max(2U,U+T+1).
For the rooted triangle, the root-out optimum is max(U,T+2), so its
double has optimum at least 2 max(U,T+2). The tested witnesses below
already suffice without determining W.

2. All single switches from the retained sharp 24-vertex parent

A switch removes two vertex-disjoint edges ab,cd and adds either ac,bd
or ad,bc, requiring simplicity and connectivity. Exhausting these choices
at the fixed labelled 24-vertex parent produces 983 distinct labelled
graphs. This is a neighbourhood, NOT a census of 24-vertex cubic graphs
and NOT 983 pairwise nonisomorphic graphs.

For every edge of every resulting graph, directly checked witnesses give
  U>=12, and either U>=14 or T>=12.
There are 35,388 parent-edge cases, including eight bridges. Hence
  max(2U,U+T+1)>=25
and
  2 max(U,T+2)>=28>27.
Both rooted constructions therefore satisfy the half bound at every
tested edge. This excludes the original targeted bad profile on this
entire switch neighbourhood.

The certificates come from cycle-space enumeration, but the exclusion
does not depend on its upper bounds: verify.py regenerates the complete
switch neighbourhood and checks the positive selected sets directly.
The compressed certificate file retains a witness for each bound.

3. Two-edge-cut path composition

Let H_1,H_2 be disjoint graphs with distinguished vertices r_i,x_i of
degree two, every other vertex cubic. Form G by adding r_1r_2 and x_1x_2.
For each i, define P_i as the largest size of a vertex set S_i containing
r_i,x_i such that the induced degrees in H_i[S_i] are one at r_i,x_i
and two at every other selected vertex. Such an induced subgraph is one
r_i--x_i path together with zero or more disjoint cycles.

Proposition. The maximum size of a good set in G containing both ends
of r_1r_2 is exactly P_1+P_2.

Proof. A good set containing those ends contains the edge r_1r_2 on
one of its cycles. A cycle crosses a cut an even number of times; as
the cut has only two edges, it must also use x_1x_2. Restricting to each
H_i gives exactly the endpoint degree conditions defining P_i, proving
the upper bound. Conversely, take an optimal set on each side and add
the two joining edges. Every selected vertex now has induced degree two.
There are no further edges between the sides, so no extra chords arise.
Their union is good and has size P_1+P_2. QED.

In particular, if P_i>=|H_i|/2 for both sides, this construction satisfies
the original half bound through either cut edge. This is a conditional
composition lemma; no claim is made that arbitrary two-poles have this
path-capacity property.

4. Application: bypassing the bridge in the sharp 26-vertex double

The retained 26-vertex example is the bridge join of two copies of P13,
the Franklin edge-subdivision cap. Subdivide one edge inside each cap
and join the two new vertices. The old bridge and the new joining edge
now form a two-edge cut of a 28-vertex cubic graph.

For each of the 19 possible subdivision edges in P13, exhaustive local
enumeration computes the path capacity between the original root and
the new subdivision vertex. The capacity distribution is
  P=8: 3 edges; P=9: 4 edges; P=10: 12 edges.
Only 4,096 subsets per edge need checking because the two endpoints
must be selected. Full witnesses are saved in cap_path_certificates.json.

The composition proposition gives T>=16 for all 19^2 bypass choices,
whereas the original half bound asks for 14 vertices. Thus this attempt
to turn a rigid bridge into a problematic non-bridge edge has at least
two vertices of surplus. It cannot produce an original counterexample.

The caps have six edge orbits. The 21 unordered pairs of representatives
were also evaluated directly. Their saved witnesses give U>=14 and
T>=16 at the old bridge. Thus the subdivision and triangle rooted doubles
at that distinguished edge satisfy their required bounds of 29 and 31.
This last statement concerns that edge, not every edge of each bypass.

5. What remains unproved

The bounded searches do not establish the universal implication
  U=h+1 and T<=h-1  ==>  W>=h-1.
They also do not cover the general subdivision obstruction or arbitrary
bridgeless cubic graphs. There is no new general lower bound here.
The original conjecture is still the unresolved step; these exclusions
must not be presented as evidence that its proof is now close.
