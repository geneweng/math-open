DIRECT ORIGINAL-COUNTEREXAMPLE SEARCH: BIPARTITE ORDER 26
29 October 2026

Outcome: no original counterexample and no proof of the original conjecture.
The attempt ended at a wall-time guard; it did not establish infeasibility.

Scope
-----
The model describes connected simple cubic bipartite graphs with 13
vertices in each part. There is no seed-distance or gadget restriction.
It fixes the neighbours of one left vertex and one adjacent right vertex
as a labelling convention. Any such graph admits these labels: choose
an edge, label its ends first in their respective parts, then label their
other two neighbours second and third on the opposite sides.

Every induced 2-regular set in a bipartite graph has even cardinality.
Thus a counterexample at order 26 would have c_ind<=12. The exact oracle
enumerates all good sets of size at least 14. Each discovered set adds
a constraint saying that it cannot remain good in the next proposed graph.
Disconnected proposed graphs would receive valid connectivity cuts.

Results
-------
There were 11 connected proposals and 2,769 forbidden good sets. Every
proposal had exact optimum 18. The run stopped after about 70.75 seconds;
the nominal 60-second guard is checked between iterations, so the last
solver/oracle/cut-encoding iteration can exceed it. No UNSAT conclusion
was reached and no untested graph is excluded by this reported outcome.

All candidates attain the maximum possible optimum in this class. If S
is good in a cubic graph, its cut has |S| edges, while the complement has
capacity at most 3(n-|S|). Hence |S|<=3n/4. For n=26 this gives at most
19 vertices, and bipartite parity reduces the upper bound to 18. Therefore
the observed optima were six vertices above the counterexample target,
with no improvement across the run. The growing constraint count is not
evidence that the search was nearing a counterexample or a proof.

Verification
------------
A separate C++ cycle-space enumeration checked all 16,384 cycle vectors
for each graph, agreeing with the Python degree-propagation oracle on
the maximum and every good-set count at sizes at least 14. The verifier
also checks connectedness, cubicity, bipartition, the labelling convention,
all 2,769 selected sets, uniqueness, and exclusion of prior good-set cuts.
There are 180,224 independently enumerated cycle vectors in total.

Reproduce:
  python3 cubic_conjecture_search/direct_bipartite/search.py
  python3 cubic_conjecture_search/direct_bipartite/verify.py
Before verifying a new run, regenerate cycle_space_checks.jsonl by running
the existing exploratory/odd_factor_enumerate.cpp executable on the saved
candidate graph6 strings in order. The search uses Z3; verification does
not. The saved run and independent verification are retained separately.

Assessment
----------
This direct synthesis attempt did not close any general proof gap. It
supplies neither a counterexample nor an exhaustive result at order 26.
Together with the earlier restricted-family results, it leaves the user's
requested stopping condition unmet. The paper/PDF is unchanged.
